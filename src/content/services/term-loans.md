mistakes:

documents:
- Last 3 years' financial statements and ITRs

faqs:
- question: How long can the tenure be?
  answer: It depends on the asset, the lender and your cash flows. We propose a tenure that the business can service comfortably, then test it against each lender's policy.
- question: What is a moratorium?
  answer: A period at the start of the loan with no instalments, or interest only, while the asset starts earning. Lenders allow it case by case.
- question: Can you move my existing loan to another lender?
  answer: Yes, where it makes sense. We compare the full cost of switching, including charges, before recommending a takeover.
- question: Do I need collateral?
  answer: Often the funded asset is the main security, and some lenders ask for more. For eligible micro and small enterprises, CGTMSE cover can reduce the need for collateral.
