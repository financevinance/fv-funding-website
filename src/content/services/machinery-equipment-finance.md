assess:
- title: The machine and supplier
  text: Quotation, supplier standing, and for used machines an independent valuation.
- title: Earning case
  text: How the machine adds output or saves cost, and how that pays the instalments.
- title: Promoter contribution
  text: The share of the cost you bring in.
- title: Track record
  text: Existing business performance and banking.
- title: Security
  text: Hypothecation of the machine, plus any collateral the lender asks for.
- title: Installation and use
  text: Where it will be installed and when production starts.
help:
- title: Check the case
  text: Whether the machine's earnings support the loan you have in mind.
- title: Plan any subsidy
  text: Eligibility for capital or interest subsidy checked before purchase.
- title: Prepare the file
  text: Quotations, projections and documents in the lender's format.
- title: Coordinate payment
  text: Disbursement to the supplier and follow-up to installation.
mistakes:
- Paying the full advance to the supplier before the loan is sanctioned
- Buying used machinery without a valuation the lender will accept
- Missing a subsidy because the purchase was made before applying
documents:
- Last 3 years' financial statements and ITRs
- Last 12 months' bank statements (all current accounts)
- GST returns for the last 12 months
- Quotation or proforma invoice for the machinery
- Valuation report (for used machinery)
- Sanction letters and repayment schedules of existing loans
- KYC of the business and promoters
faqs:
- question: Can I finance used machinery?
  answer: Yes, many lenders fund used machinery, usually on the basis of an independent valuation and the machine's remaining life.
- question: Is the machine enough security?
  answer: It is often the primary security. Some lenders ask for more; for eligible micro and small enterprises, CGTMSE cover can help.
- question: When should I apply for a subsidy?
  answer: Check eligibility before buying. Some schemes have conditions tied to timing and documentation.
- question: Who receives the loan money?
  answer: Usually the lender pays the supplier directly against the invoice.
