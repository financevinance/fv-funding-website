faqs:
- question: What is the difference between cash credit and overdraft?
  answer: Both are revolving limits. Cash credit is usually drawn against stock and receivables; an overdraft is often backed by property, deposits or other assets. The right one depends on what your business holds.
- question: How is the size of the limit decided?
  answer: Lenders look at your turnover, operating cycle, projections and security. Each lender applies its own method; we size the request so it holds up under theirs.
- question: Can a newer business get a working capital limit?
  answer: Sometimes. Lenders weigh track record heavily. In the first call we tell you plainly where you stand and what would strengthen the case.
- question: Is collateral always needed?
  answer: Not always. For eligible micro and small enterprises, credit guarantee cover such as CGTMSE can support collateral-free limits. Eligibility and the lender's policy decide.
- question: Do you help with renewal?
  answer: Yes. Limits are reviewed periodically, usually every year. We prepare the renewal file so the limit is kept or enhanced, not cut.
