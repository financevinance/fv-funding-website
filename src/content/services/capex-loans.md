faqs:
- question: How much of the cost will a lender fund?
  answer: Lenders expect a share of the cost from you, called margin or promoter contribution. The share varies by lender and project; we plan it upfront so there are no surprises.
- question: Is the loan released in one go?
  answer: Usually not. CAPEX loans are commonly released in stages as work progresses, against certificates or invoices.
- question: Can a subsidy reduce my cost?
  answer: Some central and Gujarat schemes offer capital or interest subsidy to eligible units. We check eligibility alongside the loan.
- question: Do I need a project report?
  answer: For larger CAPEX, yes. We prepare it as part of the file.
