Most declined business loans are not declined because the business is bad. They are declined because of how the request reached the lender. The fixes are rarely complicated, but they have to happen before the file goes in, not after.

## 1. The request went to the wrong lender

Every bank and NBFC has its own appetite: the sectors it likes, the ticket sizes it prefers, the security it insists on, the minimum vintage it accepts. A file that one lender would approve can be a poor fit for the next. Sending the same file to several lenders at once does not solve this. It usually adds credit enquiries to your report and makes the next lender more cautious.

**The fix:** shortlist lenders whose stated criteria you already meet, and approach them in order.

## 2. The product doesn't match the need

Working capital asked for as a term loan, or a machine funded out of a cash credit limit, creates a mismatch the credit officer will notice. Long-term assets need long-term money; the day-to-day cycle needs a revolving limit.

**The fix:** separate the need into its parts first. One request can carry more than one facility.

## 3. The numbers tell a different story in different places

Lenders read your audited financials, GST returns and bank statements side by side. When turnover in one doesn't reconcile with the others, the question is no longer whether you can repay. It becomes which set of numbers is true.

**The fix:** reconcile the three before applying, and explain any genuine difference in writing.

## 4. The repayment doesn't fit the cash flow

A loan can be affordable on paper and still fail the lender's coverage test once existing instalments are counted. Tenure chosen to save interest often makes this worse.

**The fix:** work out what the business can service comfortably, then size the loan and tenure around it.

## 5. The file is incomplete

Missing sanction letters of existing loans, an outdated stock statement, KYC with a mismatch in names or addresses: each one stalls the file and some quietly end it.

**The fix:** prepare the full document set before the first conversation with the lender.

---

None of this guarantees a sanction. Lenders decide. But a file that is matched to the right lender, asks for the right product and tells one consistent story gives a sound business its best chance of a yes.
